## 5. Customer Experience — What Your Customers See

When a customer messages your WhatsApp number for the first time, here's what happens — automatically, within seconds:

```
1. Customer sees your number on Google / Instagram / website
   → Sends "Hi, I want to plan a Goa trip in June"

2. AI receives the message and instantly replies:
   "Hi! 👋 We have 3 great Goa packages for June:
    1) Beach Bliss (4N/5D) — ₹12,500/person
    2) Goa Explorer (5N/6D) — ₹15,800/person
    3) Luxury Goa (6N/7D) — ₹22,000/person
    Which one would you like to know more about?"

3. Customer asks follow-up questions → AI answers using your business info
4. AI captures the customer's name, phone, and intent → saved as a lead
5. If customer wants to book → AI flags it as a "hot lead" → your team gets notified
6. Your staff opens the dashboard, sees the chat history, takes over to close the deal
```

**Result:** Every enquiry is captured. No customer waits more than a few seconds. No lead is forgotten.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use my existing personal WhatsApp number?**
No. WhatsApp Business API requires a dedicated number that's not active on regular WhatsApp. You'll need to provide a new number (a new SIM, landline, or virtual number works).

**Q: What if the AI gives a wrong answer?**
You retrain it. The dashboard will let us update the AI's knowledge anytime your services or pricing change.

**Q: Can I take over from the AI mid-conversation?**
Yes. Open the chat in the dashboard, type a manual reply — the AI will pause and let you handle it.

**Q: What if a customer messages outside business hours?**
The AI replies instantly 24/7. If the query is complex, the AI says: "Our team will get back to you in the morning" — and your staff sees the lead at 9 AM.

**Q: Will customers know they're talking to an AI?**
Up to you. We can make the AI introduce itself as "Traventions AI Assistant" or just respond as part of the team. Most clients prefer the latter — feels more human.

**Q: Can I send marketing campaigns to all my past customers?**
Yes — but only via Meta-approved **Marketing templates**, and only to customers who have given consent (opted in via your website or a previous chat).

**Q: What happens to the data after 15 months if I don't renew maintenance?**
The platform keeps running. You keep all your data. You just won't get new updates or our active monitoring.

**Q: Can multiple staff use the dashboard at the same time?**
Yes — unlimited concurrent users. Each staff member has their own login.

**Q: Is the platform GDPR / data protection compliant?**
Yes. All customer data is stored in Appwrite (encrypted at rest, EU-region option available). We don't share your data with anyone.
